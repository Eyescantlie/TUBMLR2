Frequently asked questions.

What happens after I submit a report?+

Your report is sent privately to our moderation team, who review it and follow up where needed.

Can I report something from a private message?+

Yes. Include only the information needed for the safety team to understand the concern.

How can I protect my account?+

Use a unique password, turn on two-factor authentication when available, avoid suspicious links, and never share verification codes.

How do I contact support?+

Use the live chat button on this page to talk with our support team.
